# Demo Script

Date: 2026-04-06
Owner: Codex

## Goal

Show a coherent RFQ-to-negotiation workflow with supporting knowledge, complaint context, and clear decision support.

## Presenter Setup

- Open the app at `/app`
- Sign in
- Start at `Dashboard`
- If the workspace is empty, click `Load Demo Workspace`

## Walkthrough

### 1. Dashboard

What to click:
- `Dashboard`
- `Load Demo Workspace` if needed

What to say:
- “This dashboard is the command center. It gives us active negotiation load, blocked quotes, revenue in play, complaint pressure, and recent working records.”

Expected outcome:
- Metrics populate
- Recent cases appear
- Recent knowledge files appear
- The portfolio brief explains what matters now

Value demonstrated:
- The product is not just a parser. It is an operating workspace with portfolio-level visibility.

### 2. Intake Path

What to click:
- `Chat Intake`

What to say:
- “Users can start from pasted email context, uploaded RFQ files, or mailbox sync. The key point is that intake turns messy inbound demand into structured case records.”

Expected outcome:
- Upload area, pasted email field, mailbox sync controls, progress state, and activity timeline are visible

Value demonstrated:
- Fast record creation and visible processing state

### 3. Case Workshop

What to click:
- `Case Workspace`
- Open `QC-DEMO-001`

What to say:
- “Here the system normalizes the RFQ into product details, summary, risks, and open issues. This is where the team sees what was extracted and what still needs a decision.”

Expected outcome:
- Structured product specs
- AI summary
- main risks
- unresolved issues
- recommendation card

Value demonstrated:
- Extraction is visible and actionable, not trapped in backend logs

### 4. Knowledge Support

What to click:
- Stay on `QC-DEMO-001` and then open `Quote Builder`

What to say:
- “Before we quote, the system ties the case to standards and pricing references already stored in the knowledge base.”

Expected outcome:
- Knowledge summary appears in the quote workspace
- Supporting files are named
- Recommended status is visible

Value demonstrated:
- Knowledge is connected to the live commercial workflow, not isolated

### 5. Quote Builder

What to click:
- `Quote Builder`
- Select `QC-DEMO-001`

What to say:
- “This is the commercial working view. We can review line-level pricing, flags, draft history, formal email output, and PDF package generation.”

Expected outcome:
- Saved quote record is visible
- Current draft and history switcher load
- Decision recommendation, line items, terms, and quote actions render

Value demonstrated:
- The tool produces approval-ready commercial output, not generic AI commentary

### 6. Negotiation And Follow-Up

What to click:
- `Negotiation`

What to say:
- “Once a quote is sent, the workflow continues. Negotiation records track what needs follow-up, what was won or lost, and what can be automated.”

Expected outcome:
- `QC-DEMO-001` appears as an active follow-up item
- Follow-up composer is visible
- Send-now and schedule actions are present when email delivery is configured

Value demonstrated:
- This is a closed-loop workflow, not a one-time quote generator

### 7. Complaint Context

What to click:
- `Customer Complaints`
- Open `Tube OD mismatch on prior shipment`

What to say:
- “Commercial decisions don’t happen in isolation. Complaint history is retained and can inform how we position the next quote or follow-up.”

Expected outcome:
- Complaint record, summary, attachment count, and email context render

Value demonstrated:
- Cross-functional context is preserved in one workspace

### 8. Finish With Recommendation

What to click:
- Return to `Dashboard` or back to `Quote Builder`

What to say:
- “The system gives a clear so-what: what the buyer needs, what is supported, what needs a decision, and what commercial action comes next.”

Expected outcome:
- Presenter can restate:
  - quote readiness
  - supporting standards/pricing evidence
  - open clarification
  - follow-up plan

Value demonstrated:
- The product reduces commercial risk while keeping the team moving.

## Fast Happy Path

1. Login
2. Dashboard
3. Load Demo Workspace
4. Open `QC-DEMO-001`
5. Review quote in `Quote Builder`
6. Show `Negotiation`
7. Show `Customer Complaints`
8. Close on dashboard summary
